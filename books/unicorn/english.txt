Hello, everyone on Reddit. I know what you're thinking, and no, this isn't a joke; it's real. I'm a deputy with the sheriff's office in Cedar Grove, New Jersey, and I found a unicorn.

That's right. A unicorn. I'm not crazy. I found a FUCKING unicorn! And unicorns are nothing like the ones in fairy tales…

Let me tell you what happened from the beginning. It was very late at night, and I was about to finish my shift when the police station received a call. A woman had been found stabbed in the forest near the local highway.

Two other officers and I immediately arrived at the scene, where state police and paramedics were already present. The caller said that some kind of animal had killed the woman, so we thought it was just a case of bad luck.

The next evening, we received another similar call: a man had been found dead on a dirt road in the forest. We thought an animal had killed him too, possibly the same animal that killed the woman the day before. We contacted the nature conservation department. They said they would investigate, but in the end, they did nothing.

On the third night, after another person was found dead, we decided to search for the animal together with staff from the conservation department. I know this sounds strange, but there hadn't been animal attacks like these since the 1970s, so we thought we needed to deal with the problem quickly.

We searched all through the forest, but still couldn't find the animal. At two in the morning, we decided to stop searching. However, on the way back to the car, I found a strange, sparkling substance on the ground that looked like glittering blood.

A week passed without any more murders, until one morning a little girl was found dead in the middle of the street where she lived, right in the center of town. Her older sister insisted that a unicorn had attacked the girl while the two sisters were playing. I dismissed that story, but then I learned I was wrong when I received a distress call over the radio. An officer had been attacked by the animal.

My fellow officers and I rushed into the forest to the place where the officer had been attacked. We found him lying beside letters written in sparkling blood that spelled out the message: “Leave me alone.”

After that, we closed off the forest and organized patrols around the area. We searched every cave, every hole, and every inch of the forest, but still couldn't find anything.

Then, when I was alone, I saw it. A unicorn. At that moment, it was both incredibly beautiful and terribly frightening. I pulled out my handgun and shot at it, but the bullets didn't seem to hurt it, even though it was bleeding sparkling blood.

I dove behind a rock when the unicorn missed me: its horn had almost gone through my chest. It kept chasing me as I rolled around and squeezed my way through the bushes.

Finally, I ran to a nature conservation station, where two forest rangers were on duty. I told them I was being chased, but they didn't believe me until one of them was suddenly attacked by the unicorn and its horn pierced right through him. The second ranger tried to run away, but that majestic animal trampled him to death.

I stood frozen, not knowing what to do. The unicorn looked at me and growled, then galloped back into the forest.

Several days have passed without any more murders. No one believes that the killer was a damn unicorn. I'm writing this in the hope that someone knows something about what happened, and so everyone knows they must stay away from the Cedar Grove Reserve.
